143 Marine
The World Is Water
143 Marine is an evolving collection of data, tools, stories, and resources centered around life on and around the water.

The name is inspired by the long-standing maritime association of "143" with the phrase "I Love You." To us, 143 Marine represents a love for the waterways, communities, industries, recreation, and traditions that connect people through water.

Our Philosophy
Water connects people in many different ways.

Some fish.

Some sail.

Some dive.

Some work on ports, vessels, and waterfronts.

Some simply enjoy spending time near lakes, rivers, and coastlines.

143 Marine exists to explore and support those connections.

Current Projects
Lake Atlas
A structured collection of lakes, reservoirs, rivers, species information, access points, and water-related resources.

Access Atlas
An expanding database of public launches, ramps, marinas, shoreline access points, and recreational opportunities.

Water Conditions
Research and development focused on visibility forecasting, water clarity, temperature trends, environmental conditions, and outdoor planning tools.

Community Intelligence
Future contributor-driven observations and local reporting intended to improve the accuracy and usefulness of water condition forecasts.

Long-Term Vision
We believe useful tools create stronger communities.

Our goal is to build a platform where water users can:

Discover places to explore
Find public access opportunities
Understand environmental conditions
Share observations
Learn from one another
Support water-based recreation and conservation
Related Initiatives
Lifetime Fishing Fund
The Lifetime Fishing Fund is a separate mission focused on helping people gain long-term access to fishing opportunities through lifetime licensing and outdoor access initiatives.

143 Marine supports awareness of the water resources, recreation, and communities that make those opportunities meaningful.

Project Status
This project is actively evolving.

Datasets, schemas, tools, forecasts, and website features are expected to change as the platform grows.

Contributions, ideas, corrections, and observations are always welcome.
